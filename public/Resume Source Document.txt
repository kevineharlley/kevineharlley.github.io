Career Narrative (Long Version)
My career has been defined by a consistent focus on translating complex requirements into practical technology solutions. Beginning in information technology and infrastructure support in higher education, I developed a foundation in systems administration, customer support, networking and operational technology.
Early experiences in scientific research and enterprise IT further exposed me to data analysis, software development, information security, compliance and business operations. These opportunities broadened my understanding of how technology can be leveraged to solve real organizational challenges across a range of diverse environments.
As my technical capabilities matured, I expanded into application and product development in different environments, contributing to the development of customer-facing applications, analytics platforms, and emerging technology solutions. This path eventually led to a software engineering internship at Google, where I contributed to an open-source compiler and high-level synthesis technology that aimed to bridge the gap between software development and specialized hardware infrastructure.
Building on my technical and business experience, I transitioned into enterprise technology consulting at Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu, supporting global ERP and CPQ implementations valued at several million dollars. In these roles, I operated at the intersection of business and technology, helping business stakeholders and development teams organize and modernize Configure-to-Order processes, implement enterprise platforms and align technological investments with strategic business objectives.
Presently, I continue to work as a Configuration Engineer/Developer where I serve as product owner, systems administrator, and solutions architect for my organization’s Configure-to-Order platforms supporting millions of dollars in annual revenue. My work focuses on translating product, manufacturing, and business requirements into scalable software solutions while driving digital transformation initiatives, platform modernization efforts and enterprise system optimization.
Across every stage of my career, the common thread has been a passion for connecting people, processes, and technology to solve complex problems and deliver measurable business value.
